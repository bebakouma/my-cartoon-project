Final Script: Abraham Meets Melchizedek
[EXT. OAKS OF MAMRE – DAY]
(Birds chirp. Sunlight filters through the trees.)

NARRATOR (gentle tone):
Long ago, under the big oak trees, Abraham was resting when a visitor appeared…

ABRAHAM (smiling):
Hello, friend! I’m Abraham. Who are you?

MELCHIZEDEK (offering bread and wine):
Peace be with you, Abraham. I’m Melchizedek, priest of the Most High God. I bring you blessings.

ABRAHAM (grateful):
Thank you, Melchizedek. May God bless you, too!

NARRATOR:
And under the oaks, a special friendship began—with kindness and blessing.
